Basant Maheshwari

Distinguished Professor at Western Sydney University. Director of the Australia India Water Centre. Leader of the MARVI programme.

Thirty years of research on water and its interactions with the landscape — groundwater, irrigation, river health and urban water — built on a conviction that the people who draw from an aquifer are the ones who can manage it. MARVI turned that into method: village volunteers monitoring their own wells, and a shared record that makes a common resource visible to everyone drawing on it.

Position

Current focus.

Distinguished Professor in water, environment and sustainability at the School of Science, Western Sydney University, and Director of the Australia India Water Centre — a bilateral centre connecting researchers, government, industry and communities across Australia and India through research, education, training and outreach.

The current work runs along three lines: groundwater monitoring and village-scale managed aquifer recharge; transdisciplinary training for the next generation of water professionals; and the water quality, urban water and irrigation questions that decide whether a catchment stays liveable.
